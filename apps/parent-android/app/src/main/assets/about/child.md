# About Harbor on this phone

Harbor Family connects this phone to your parents' Harbor app. This page explains everything it does. It is the same for everyone, and you can come back to it any time.

## What Harbor shares with your parents
- Your location. About every 5 minutes while you are moving and every 15 minutes while you are still, when Android allows. Each update is latitude, longitude, how accurate it is and the time. Your parents can see recent places for up to 30 days. A notification stays on this phone while location is being shared.
- Your screen time. Which apps you used and for how long over the last 7 days, and the names of the apps on this phone you can open. Your parents see the last report your phone sent, which can be a few minutes old. Up to 30 days of reports are kept.
- Possible concerns in messages. Only the kind of concern, the app it came from, the time, and whether the sender is saved in your contacts.
- Check-in answers. When a parent asks you to check in, they get your answer ("I'm okay" or "Running late"), the time, and the last place this phone already knew if it is less than an hour old.

## What you see in the Family tab
The Family tab shows a map of your family: the children in your family, including you, and any parent who chose to share their location. You see each person's latest place and when it was updated. There is no trail and no history, and nobody can message or call anyone from it. You can zoom in and out.

The other children in your family can see your latest place there too. A parent can turn the family map off for children at any time, and then it shows nothing. A parent who shares can stop whenever they like.

## What Harbor checks on this phone
Harbor watches new notifications from these apps, and your incoming text messages: {{monitored_apps}}.

It looks for possible concerns: bullying or harassment, self-harm or wellbeing, adult content, drugs or alcohol, violence or threats, and contact from an unknown adult. The checking happens on this phone.

When something is flagged, your parents are sent only the category, the app, the time and whether the sender is saved in your contacts. They are never sent the message text, who sent it, a phone number, or any part of the message. Harbor does not read your whole conversations.

## What your parents can control
- Your parents can pause the phone, set a daily limit on your screen time, set a bedtime, and set a limit or a block on particular apps. Harbor warns you a few minutes before an app or the day's screen time runs out.
- When an app is blocked, or a limit or bedtime has been reached, it will not open. Harbor shows you which rule stopped it, and you can ask a parent from that screen. They are sent a notification and can say yes or no.
- Some apps are never blocked, whatever the rules say: the phone, messages, Settings, the keyboard and the home screen. You can always call or text someone, including an emergency number. Time in the phone, messages and Settings still counts toward a daily limit.
- While Harbor is on, removing any app from this phone needs a parent's OK, not only Harbor. Harbor cannot tell which app is being removed, so it asks for all of them. Your parent is sent a notification and can say yes, which allows removing apps for 15 minutes. Installing apps is not affected.

## What Harbor never does
- It never records your screen, your camera or your microphone.
- Screen time never reads messages, web pages, photos or contacts.
- It never sends your parents the words of a message.

## Who can turn it off
Only your parents, by removing this phone in their Harbor app. There is no switch for it in this app. If a permission is turned off on this phone, Harbor shows it here and your parents are told that sharing has stopped.

## What Harbor can miss
Harbor makes mistakes in both directions. Muted or hidden message previews and end-to-end encrypted messages may not be seen. Updates can be delayed by battery settings or by Android's permissions. A quiet day does not mean everything is fine.

## Notifications
Harbor shows a notification while location is shared. A parent's check-in request also arrives as a notification, and always shows in the Check in tab.

## This phone's connection
Harbor stores this phone's pairing credentials securely and checks its family connection with signed requests.

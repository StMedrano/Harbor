# Terms of service

Draft for review. By using Harbor Family you agree to these terms.

## Who can use Harbor
Parents and guardians create accounts. A child uses Harbor only on a phone that a parent or guardian has set up and paired with a code.

## Your responsibilities
- Use Harbor only for children you are responsible for.
- Tell your child what Harbor shares. Harbor is built to be open with them and shows them this in the app.
- Keep your sign-in details safe, and do not share your account.
- Do not use Harbor to harass, stalk or harm anyone, or to watch an adult who has not agreed.

## What Harbor is not
- Harbor is not an emergency service. Check-ins and alerts can be late or missed. If someone may be in danger, call your local emergency number.
- Harbor does not guarantee that every concern will be found. Alerts can be wrong in both directions.
- Locations and reports can be delayed or out of date. Harbor labels information that may be old.

## What Harbor does
Harbor shares a child's location, screen time, possible-concern alerts and check-in answers with the parents in the family, as described in the Privacy policy. It does not record the screen, camera or microphone, and it does not block or limit apps.

## Ending use
A parent can remove a device at any time in the Family tab, which stops sharing from it. You can stop using Harbor at any time.

## Availability
Harbor may change, pause or stop working, and may be unavailable from time to time.

## Responsibility
Harbor is provided as is. To the extent the law allows, it is not responsible for losses that come from relying on Harbor instead of supervising directly.

## Changes
If these terms change, the new version is shown in the app.

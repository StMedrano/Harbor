# Privacy policy

Draft for review. This describes what the Harbor Family app does with information today.

## Who this covers
Harbor Family is one app. A parent or guardian creates an account and adds children. A child does not create an account: they enter a code from a parent on their own phone.

## What Harbor collects
- Parent account: your name, email address and sign-in details.
- Family: the family name, your children's display names, the devices that are paired, and who the other parents are.
- Location from a child's phone: latitude, longitude, accuracy, source and time. About every 5 minutes while moving and every 15 while still, when Android allows. Kept for up to 30 days.
- Screen time from a child's phone: which apps were used and for how long over the last 7 days, and the names of the apps that can be opened. Kept for up to 30 days. This does not read messages, web pages, photos or contacts.
- Message alerts from a child's phone: Harbor checks new notifications from {{monitored_apps}} and incoming text messages on the phone. Only the kind of concern, the app, the time and whether the sender is a saved contact are sent. The message text, the sender, phone numbers and any part of a message are never sent. Kept for up to 30 days.
- Check-ins: when a parent asks, the child's answer ("I'm okay" or "Running late"), the time, and the last place the phone already had if it is under an hour old. Kept for up to 30 days.
- Notifications: a device token so the phone can be notified, sent through Firebase Cloud Messaging.

## What Harbor does not do
- It does not record the screen, the camera or the microphone.
- It does not block or limit apps.
- It does not send message text to anyone.

## Who can see it
Parents and guardians who are active members of the family can see their children's shared information. A child can see what their own phone shares in the app's About page. Information is not shown to anyone outside the family through the app.

## Stopping sharing
A child cannot turn sharing off in the app. A parent stops it by removing the device in the Family tab. If a permission is turned off on the phone or Harbor is removed, sharing stops and the parent app shows that.

## Keeping and removing data
Shared information is removed after 30 days. Removing a device removes the information that device shared.

## How data is protected
Requests from a child's phone are signed with a key stored in the phone's secure hardware or keystore. Data is stored in a protected database and read only by parents of the family.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version is shown in the app.

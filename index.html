What Harbor does

Harbor stays visible.

Harbor helps your parent manage this Android device. In this prototype, your parent can see sample safety and device information such as location, screen time, app access, web activity, calls and message safety alerts, schedules, and emergency status.

Location & check-insShows where the device is and lets you say “I'm okay.”
Time & appsShows your limits and which apps are available, Unlimited, or locked.
Safety activityRepresents web, call, and message safety monitoring with sample data.
Emergency accessGet Help and calling a parent stay available even when time is up. Get Help alerts parents only; it does not call emergency services.

Demo only — Harbor is not actually monitoring or controlling this device.
